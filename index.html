What I couldn't confirm

Check these in game before you rely on them.

  • ○
    No transfer between bases. My assumption, about 90% sure. If items do move, the carry list shrinks.
  • ◐
    World Tree coordinates. Two guides put the Base of the World Tree fast travel point at -838, 689, in the far northwest. Two others print 841, 639 with the sign dropped, and the earlier version of this page copied that. The X sign is settled; the Y value is within about 50.
  • ◐
    Where Paloxite Ingots are made. Two guides say the Ancient Furnace. Pindrop's item page says the Production Assembly Line. All four sources agree the recipe unlocks at Lv 74.
  • ◐
    Synthesizer cost. Pindrop and palworld.tools say 50 Paloxite Ingots and 10 Cores. One guide lists 100 ingots, 10 Holy Water, 20 Thermal Cores and 20 Cryogenic Coolant.
  • ◐
    Work-suitability levels. Pindrop's structure pages list Lv 1+ for the quarries and Recycler. palworld.tools lists Lv 10 workers. Neither gives numbers for the furnaces. I removed the Lv 6+ figures the earlier version showed.
  • ○
    Structures with no level here. Ore Mining Site, Electric Furnace and Breeding Farm had no level I could verify. The planner treats them as available at any level.
  • ◐
    Fourth base. Pindrop gives base levels 8, 15 and 25 for bases 2, 3 and 4, and three other guides agree on 25. One tracker derives 21.
  • ◐
    Tech Point costs. A Palpedia table disagrees with Pindrop's per-structure pages, and the Ancient points are separate. I left costs out.
  • ◐
    Recycler output. Mythical Wood and Paloxite are guaranteed on every relic tier, with Radiant Gems at low rates and Cores from tier 4 and 5 relics. Sources don't say whether the Paloxite is ore or ingots. One guide says it also takes Holy Water.
  • ◐
    Holy Water source. One guide says Teafant Springs in the World Tree, 10 per touch. Another ties it to a quest with the World Tree Guardian.
  • ◐
    Worker cap. Every preset gives 15. The level table reaches 30 at base level 30, and the setting maxes at 50.
  • ○
    Worker counts are my estimates, recounted per structure plus a hauling allowance.
  • ○
    Merged bases. The 1, 2 and 3 base layouts are my suggestions. Nothing in the sources says what works best.
  • ◐
    Wiping bases. One page covering 1.0 (checked Aug 2026) says disassembly refunds 100% and Pal workstations auto-dismantle with the Palbox. It says base level is a separate track but never says whether it resets when the last Palbox goes. I couldn't confirm that.
